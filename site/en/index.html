4.1 · Make it yours

Due this week,
right on your desktop.

Native Mac widgets show the next seven days of assignments in deadline order. Choose small, medium or large, and click to return to the app. Data comes from your latest sync.

Enable Mac system widgets in Settings → Plugins. Then right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets and search for Canvas. Requires macOS 14 or later.

Add what you need.

AI is off by default; enable it in Settings → Plugins. Start with the clean default appearance and install themes when you want them. Existing themes are kept. Uninstalling preserves courses, settings and reports; reinstall from the bundled copy.

How do I connect my AI?+

Install Canvas Manager, sign in and sync your courses. Follow the MCP connection guide once, then connect the service to a compatible AI to query your courses in chat.

The connection has been tested with Codex. Other AI tools need custom MCP support. Queries read your local course cache; keep the desktop app running when a fresh sync is needed.

Good to know

Before you start,
a few answers.

Compatibility, sign-in and your data.

Does it support my school?+
11 Canvas school presets span six continents, including HKUST (Guangzhou), HKUST, CityUHK, NUS, SUSS, Harvard, Oxford, Melbourne, Auckland, Witwatersrand and Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. Official entry points are verified; the new campuses still require real-account testing. Version 4.1 lets you select a school and Chinese or English in the welcome guide or settings. Sign-in, MFA, course access and downloads may vary by account. Other schools and learning platforms are not currently promised.
Will it run on my Mac?+
The Apple Silicon build supports M-series chips such as M1, M2, M3 and M4. Check your chip under Apple menu → About This Mac. Requires macOS 13 or later. An Intel Mac build is not currently available.
How do I install it on Windows?+
Download the complete EXE installer for Windows 10/11 x64 and double-click it. It installs for your current user and opens automatically. You can then launch it from the desktop or Start menu. Select Sync and sign in with your school account. The installer is unsigned, so Windows may display a first-run warning.
What if macOS cannot verify the developer?+
This build is locally signed and has not been signed with an Apple Developer certificate or notarized. After verifying the download source and attempting to open the app, check System Settings → Privacy & Security for an Open Anyway option. The exact message depends on your macOS version.

If macOS specifically reports malware or a damaged file, stop and report the issue. For more information, see Apple's guide to opening apps safely ↗。
Do I need another account?+
No separate account is needed. Select Sync and sign in through your school's login page. Your school account must have access to the relevant Canvas courses.
How are my data and AI requests handled?+
Course caches, downloaded files and settings stay on your computer. Sync connects to your school's Canvas. If you use AI features, the relevant course or assignment content is sent to your selected AI provider. AI is off by default. Enable it and review provider settings under Settings → Plugins. Third-party AI providers may charge separately.
Is this an official school or Canvas product?+
Canvas Manager is an independent tool for university Canvas users. It is not an official product of any school or Instructure Canvas. Your school's Canvas remains the source of truth. For help, visit the project feedback page ↗。
